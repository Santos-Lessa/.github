## About

**Santos Lessa** is a family technology company focused on building practical solutions for real businesses.

We work at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, software engineering and digital products**, helping companies modernize their operations, automate processes and create better digital experiences.

Our approach is simple: understand the business first, then build the technology that actually makes sense for it.

**Artificial Intelligence**

Custom AI systems, intelligent automation, agents, forecasting, knowledge systems and AI powered internal tools.

**Software**

Modern web applications, internal platforms, APIs and custom business software.

**Digital Products**
